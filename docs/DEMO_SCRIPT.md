# Five minute demonstration

0:00 Open Incident workspace. Explain that all bundled footage is synthetic and that the demo works offline.

0:30 Open the counter incident. Play the clip and toggle detections. Point to the temporal score curve and selected keyframes.

1:00 Expand the rejected knife claim. Show that person and bag detections do not support a knife. Toggle the raw-output comparison, then return to the ledger.

1:45 Open the intent claim. Explain why the system downgrades it. Click a severity factor and show its source claims. State that reference weights are uncalibrated.

2:20 Show the policy vocabulary badge. Confirm a review with a note. Open the audit trail and demonstrate that the action was recorded, not dispatched.

2:50 Run the sample through Pipeline trace. Expand the structured stage outputs. Explain that M2 and M6 research training is separate from the sample baseline.

3:30 Open Benchmarks. Report actual synthetic checks, then point out all pending research results. Do not compare the sample rejection rate to detector AUC.

4:00 Open Annotation. Enter factors, score, actions and an evidence span in a practice session. Explain independent overlap and adjudication.

4:40 Show the architecture and exclusions. End with the remaining study work: licensed data, real model runs, independent ratings, calibration and held-out evaluation.
